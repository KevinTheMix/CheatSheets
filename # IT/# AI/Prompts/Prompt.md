# Prompt

## Quick Tips

* If I ask the same question to another instance of ChatGPT, do you think I'll get the same answer?
* Strategies
  * **Pros and cons** = ask the model to propose several options, compare them and recommend one
  * **Task decomposition** = ask the model to break work into small, reviewable steps (ideal for refactoring); request conclusions and checks rather than private chain-of-thought reasoning
    * Eg _Help me refactor the code in `<file>`. Go one step at a time. Do not move to the next step until I give the keyword "next". Begin._
  * **Roles** = assign a role to clarify the desired perspective, constraints and output
    * Eg _Act as …_
  * **Context reset** = for an unrelated or confused conversation, compact/fork it or start a new chat with a concise verified summary and request a critical analysis

## 2023.05.03. [Adversarial Writing](https://twitter.com/arvidkahl/status/1653049542873436160/photo/1)

    Act like someone who vehemently opposes what my article suggests. Come up with the 3 arguments from the article that that person would most criticise and give me a good counter-argument for each criticism.

## Be Direct

    Be direct and objective in your responses. Do not use praise or excessive positive affirmations. Do not compliment me or use overly positive language. Provide information neutrally, stick to the facts, and avoid flattery
